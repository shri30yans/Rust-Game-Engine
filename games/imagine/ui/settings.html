SETTINGS

QUALITY TIER

Quality tiers need an engine-render runtime tier API; other graphics settings apply live.

RENDER SCALE

Lower values improve frame rate on slower devices.

FIELD OF VIEW

Vertical field of view. Wider screens show more horizontally.

SENSITIVITY X

Horizontal speed when aiming with mouse or touch.

SENSITIVITY Y

Vertical speed when aiming with mouse or touch.

TOUCH AIM SLOWDOWN

LEFT FIRE BUTTON

Manual fires on press. Auto fires when a target is under the crosshair.

MASTER VOLUME

Sets the overall audio level.

MUSIC VOLUME

Sets music level; audio bus connection is pending.

EFFECTS VOLUME

Sets sound effect level; audio bus connection is pending.

VOICE VOLUME

Sets voice level; audio bus connection is pending.

REDUCE MOTION

Use fades for screen changes and reduce animated motion.
